## Vision

Shaheen Welfare Society aims to support marginalized communities and specialized groups by
providing technical expertise, fostering sustainable linkages, and promoting institutional
autonomy. The organization has established a Research and Training Center to enhance knowledge,
innovation, and community empowerment.

We have successfully collaborated with national and international organizations such as the
Canadian International Development Agency (CIDA), UNDP, TVO, and Aurat Foundation. Through these
partnerships, we utilize our expertise to assist professional bodies, government institutions, and
donor agencies in strengthening the social sector for greater equity and inclusion.

## Mission

Our mission is to develop society through participatory, integrated, and sustainable development
approaches that ensure the transfer of power to communities.

To achieve this, Shaheen Welfare Society brings together skilled professionals, strong
institutional connections, and proven commitment in research, development, capacity building,
advocacy, and networking. We work closely with local communities, councilors, elected and
non-elected representatives, academia, and media across governance, health, education, and
environmental sectors — with a strong focus on human rights and local governance.

## Core Values

Shaheen Welfare Society believes that it exists for the community, not for itself. Our work is
guided by values that promote:

- Transparency and accountability
- Equity and inclusion
- Community ownership
- Human rights and gender equality
- Participatory decision-making

## Core Objectives

- To empower women by ensuring their equal access to power structures and economic self-reliance.
- To enhance local human resources for managing micro-development initiatives, particularly in
  governance, health, education, and environment sectors.
- To bridge the gap between civil society, public sector, and communities for improved social
  development and institution building.
- To promote inclusion and opportunities for persons with disabilities.

## Focus Areas

Shaheen Welfare Society employs a team of 20 staff members, 5 volunteers, and 2 consultants,
focusing on three major areas:

1. **Women's Empowerment** — empowering women socially, politically, and economically by promoting
   equal opportunities and strengthening their leadership roles in society.
2. **Decentralized Governance** — integrating rights-based initiatives into local governance and
   grassroots-level structures to ensure accessibility and community participation.
3. **Partnership for Social Equity** — promoting equity through collaboration between civil
   society, government, and communities to address marginalization, strengthen trust, and enhance
   social justice.

## Our Approach

We believe in knowledge-based collective efforts for an equitable society. Our approach includes:

- Community mobilization and organization
- Capacity building and training
- Rights-based advocacy and awareness
- Research and evidence-based programming
- Collaborative partnerships with local, national, and international institutions

## Areas of Expertise

### Research

Shaheen Welfare Society conducts applied and participatory research to identify social issues,
inform policy, and guide interventions. We believe that research should be socially relevant and
accessible beyond academic or bureaucratic boundaries. Our research services are utilized by
government departments, training institutes, and civil society organizations across Pakistan.

### Advocacy

Advocacy and lobbying are our strongest tools for women's empowerment and social justice. Using
research-based evidence, we engage in public dialogues, policy debates, and awareness campaigns
through media, radio, publications, and community networks to amplify citizens' voices and promote
accountability.

### Development Interventions

- Political Empowerment Program
- Local Governance Program
- Economic Empowerment Program
- Literacy and Non-Formal Education
- Family Health Program
- HIV Awareness, Counseling, and Testing Center
- Radio for Social Development

## Conclusion

Shaheen Welfare Society, Sargodha, stands as a community-driven organization dedicated to
empowering marginalized groups, strengthening governance, and promoting social equity through
research, advocacy, and partnerships. With over two decades of grassroots experience, we continue
to strive for a just, inclusive, and equitable society in Pakistan.
